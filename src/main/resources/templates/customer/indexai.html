Origins

The
Hakka Story.

Born from the vibrant cross-cultural intersections of Kolkata and Old Delhi, our culinary roots dig deep into the history of Indo-Chinese fusion. Generations of wok masters perfected the balance of fiery Indian spices and subtle Chinese techniques.

Today, Hakka Treasures stands as a professional lab dedicated to preserving that exact heritage while elevating it to a modern standard of excellence.
